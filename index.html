Questions? Answers.

How do I pay?

After the detail and walk-through. Cash is accepted; ask about Venmo when booking.

What if it rains?

We'll pick another date. No fee for weather rescheduling.

Where does it happen?

Drop-off or at your home in Prosper, TX. Drop-off directions are shared after confirmation. At-home needs a water tap and an outlet.

Is it safe for PPF, ceramic coating, or matte paint?

Tell me when you book. I check the finish and the maker's guidance before choosing products. New protection may need a waiting period.

Can you remove every stain?

No. Some old stains are permanent. I'll tell you what's realistic before starting.

Are prices fixed?

They're starting prices. SUVs and trucks add $20, and very dirty cars may need a quote. You approve the final price first.
